How it works

Four steps. No mystery. No endless meetings.

  1. 01

    We talk

    A short call or WhatsApp chat. You tell me what the business does, who your customers are, and what's frustrating online right now.

  2. 02

    I map a plan

    You get a clear proposal: what I'll build, what it will cost, and when it will be ready. No surprises, no vague "packages."

  3. 03

    I build

    I design and launch while you keep running the shop. You'll review a draft, request tweaks, and we go live when it feels right.

  4. 04

    You grow

    Optional monthly care keeps everything current. When you need a new page or a seasonal offer, you message me — I handle the rest.
